### Findings and final repairs

1. **P2 — coalescing could revive an ABA-superseded provider attempt.** The earlier `coalesce_owners_tx` unconditionally reassigned `owner_generation` to the winner's generation. An original A-generation-1 ticket, invalidated by A→B→A, could consequently pass the winner-based authorization query after ownership transfer. Final `prototype/src/store.rs:668` instead executes `UPDATE targets SET pull_owner=?,owner_generation=-1 WHERE pull_owner IN (...)`. Final `authorize_ticket` at `store.rs:1775` still requires `d.route_generation=t.owner_generation`; device generations start at zero and remain nonnegative. Repeated merges therefore cannot revive that ticket. The full ABA test at `prototype/src/control_tests.rs:455` checks rejection before and after two transfers, winner-only retained Pull/ACK, and preservation of a frozen receipt. **CLOSED in source.**

2. **P2 — transferred old jobs could send a revoked direct-ACK capability.** `prototype/src/runtime.rs:330-340` passes `&ticket.device_key` to transport; `prototype/src/transport.rs:295` writes it as `provider_device_key`. Before final fencing, a transferred target could authorize under winner B while emitting original A. Actual Android `ProviderAckIdentity.fromDirectPayload` (`pushgo-android/.../data/ChannelSubscriptionService.kt:147`) and Apple `DeliveryIdentity.direct` (`pushgo/Shared/Services/ProviderDeliveryAckFailureStore.swift:78`) consume that immutable payload key. Android `ChannelSubscriptionRepository.ackMessages` and Apple `ProviderIngressCoordinator.drainAckMarkers` send ACK to the stored destination key, with no substitution of B. Thus a newly sent direct payload could persist locally while its ACK failed to release the retained Gateway body. Final fixed `owner_generation=-1` prevents these old jobs from newly entering transport. It does not rebind an old frozen job to a new wire key. **CLOSED in source.**

The smaller final repair matches actual mainline callers: `src/dispatch/workers.rs:796` validates a claimed job's original device/platform/token through `provider_route_is_current`; a false result settles `Superseded` before send. The FCM worker performs this check around line1532. Mainline SQLite `coalesce_duplicate_provider_routes_in_tx` (`src/storage/database/sqlite/access/routes.rs:250`) transfers subscriptions and retained Pull then deletes the original device, without rebinding the frozen outbox job. Its `provider_route_is_current` at line369 looks up the original key. The previously considered authorization-returning-winner-wire-key alternative was withdrawn because it would introduce old-job rebinding rather than preserve this behavior.
